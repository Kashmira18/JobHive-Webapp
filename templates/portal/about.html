The Process

How JobHive Works

Simple steps to connect talent and opportunity

For Job Seekers
1
Create Your Profile

Sign up with Google or email. Build your candidate profile with your skills, experience, and education.

2
Discover Opportunities

Browse thousands of verified job listings. Filter by category, location, salary, and work mode.

3
Apply in One Click

Apply directly through JobHive. Track all your applications from a single dashboard.

4
Get Hired

Receive interview invitations and job offers directly. Your next career chapter starts here.

For Employers
1
Register Your Company

Submit your company details for admin verification. Approved companies get full access to the platform.

2
Post Job Listings

Use our 4-step job wizard to create detailed listings with skills, salary, and perks. Publish instantly.

3
Review Applications

Manage all incoming applications from your dashboard. Filter, shortlist, and contact candidates.

4
Hire the Best

Connect with pre-screened candidates. Make faster, smarter hiring decisions with JobHive analytics.

What We Offer

Our Services

Everything you need for seamless hiring and job searching

Job Advertising

Post jobs with detailed descriptions, skill tags, salary ranges and perks. Reach thousands of qualified candidates across Pakistan.

Smart Job Search

Advanced filters by location, category, experience level, salary, and work mode (Remote, On-site, Hybrid) help candidates find the perfect fit fast.

Verified Companies

Every company goes through our admin approval process before posting jobs — so candidates always apply to legitimate, verified employers.

Smart Dashboards

Candidates track applications and get job recommendations. Companies manage postings, view applicant stats, and filter talent — all in one place.

Google OAuth Login

Candidates can sign in instantly with their Google account — no lengthy registration, just one click and you're in.

Premium Plans

Companies can upgrade to Pro or Enterprise plans for featured listings, unlimited postings, advanced analytics, and dedicated support.
